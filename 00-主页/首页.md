## 最近更新

```dataview

  file.link AS "笔记",
  dateformat(file.mtime, "MM-dd HH:mm") AS "更新时间"
FROM ""
WHERE !startswith(file.path, ".") AND file.name != "首页"
SORT file.mtime DESC
LIMIT 8
```
